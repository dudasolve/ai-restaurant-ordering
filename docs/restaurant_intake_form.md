# New Restaurant Intake Form — question list (v1)

To be implemented as a shareable form (tool TBD — see open decisions). One submission per restaurant. ~20 minutes.

## Section 1 — Business
1. Legal business name (as registered with the IRS)
2. DBA / restaurant brand name
3. EIN (required for SMS carrier registration — orders confirmations by text)
4. Owner/decision-maker name, mobile, email
5. Business website URL

## Section 2 — Locations (repeat per store taking AI orders)
6. Store address
7. Store public phone number (the one customers call today)
8. Hours (per day)
9. Will THIS store take AI phone orders? (yes/no — stores not enrolled are info-only)

## Section 3 — Phone system
10. Who provides your phone service? (carrier like AT&T/Verizon · VoIP like Cronus/RingCentral · not sure)
11. Do you have access to your provider's customer portal? (yes/no/not sure)
12. How do you want the AI to receive calls? (all calls · only when line is busy/unanswered · only during set hours · not sure, recommend for me)

## Section 4 — Menu
13. Do you have online ordering? Paste the URL (we import the menu automatically from it)
14. If no online ordering: we'll send a spreadsheet template — who fills it? (name/email)
15. Do prices differ between locations? (yes/no)
16. Items you do NOT want sold by phone (e.g., catering, alcohol)

## Section 5 — AI agent personality
17. Assistant name (e.g., "Bea") — or "suggest one"
18. Greeting: exact first sentence customers should hear (or "use standard")
19. Tone: warm & friendly · efficient & professional · playful
20. Languages: English only · +Spanish · +Arabic · other
21. Top 10 questions customers call about (free text — becomes the FAQ knowledge)

## Section 6 — Operations
22. Who monitors incoming orders? (name + role)
23. On what device? (tablet · phone · computer)
24. Default pickup-ready time to quote (e.g., 15–20 min)
25. Rejection reasons to offer as presets (default: kitchen too busy, item unavailable, closing soon, temporarily unable)
26. Phone number to receive callback/escalation alerts

## Section 7 — Sign-offs
27. SMS opt-in script approval (standard text shown, checkbox)
28. Confirmation: menu prices reviewed before go-live is mandatory (checkbox)
